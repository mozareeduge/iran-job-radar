# Privacy and safety

- Everything stays on your computer: your profile, the jobs found, your notes and your saved/hidden marks. They are kept in the `workspace/` folder (SQLite and JSON). Nothing is uploaded.
- The radar contacts only the job sites listed in the README, plus a font from jsDelivr when you open the report.
- `serve` listens on 127.0.0.1 only.
- The radar never applies for a job, never logs in, and never sends your data anywhere.

To report a problem, open a GitHub issue. Do not post personal data in it.
